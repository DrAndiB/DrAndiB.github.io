Browser game · Up to four players on one keyboard

Tabletop Turbo

A homage to the tabletop racing games of the 1990s, where tiny toy cars race across the furniture and four friends crowd around one keyboard. Sixteen miniature worlds in four cups, from a breakfast table and a garden to a model railway, a Mars diorama, a pinball machine, a haunted dollhouse and the open works of a watch, raced in cars, rovers, speedboats, hovercraft, snowmobiles, monster trucks and luxury SUVs. Each world has its own hazards: spilt jam and ink, whirlpools, level crossings, Bunsen flames, trapdoors and turning gears. In head-to-head mode the screen follows the leader, and whoever drops off its edge is out. There are also lap races, cup championships with trophies, and a time trial against your own ghost car.

Play Tabletop Turbo →

Best on a computer. Players use the arrow keys, WASD, IJKL and the numpad, and the keys can be changed in the menu. With three or four players, “Easy keys” needs only two keys each. Gamepads work too, and on a phone you can race alone with touch steering.

Interactive map · Ancient history

Roman News Isochrones

How many days did a message need to cross the Roman Empire? Pick a starting city, a messenger and a season, and the map colours the Empire by travel time. Tap a city to see the fastest route by road and sea. The model is checked against journeys recorded by Cicero, Plutarch, Tacitus and Pliny.

Field app · Birding · The 2012 app, ported 1:1

AndroBird (2012)

In 2012 my brother and I built AndroBird, an Android app for recording bird and amphibian sightings. This is the same app rebuilt for the browser. Point your phone at the bird, estimate the distance, pick the species, and AndroBird places the sighting on the map using GPS and the compass. It covers more than 1,400 species with German names and exports to MiniAvi and CSV. You can install it on a phone and it works offline.

Open AndroBird (2012) →

The app is in German, like the original. It works best on a phone with GPS and a compass. On a computer, set the direction by dragging the compass, or long-press the map to add a sighting. Your sightings stay in your browser; only the map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap or Esri.

Field app · Birding · 2026 edition

AndroBird 2026

The same idea, rebuilt with what a phone can do in 2026. Raise the phone and the bearing comes from the camera, corrected for magnetic declination. Say or type “3 red kites flying over” and the form fills itself in. It records survey walks with a GPS track, uses the European breeding codes, can blur the nest sites of sensitive species, and exports to eBird, GPX and GeoJSON as well as MiniAvi and CSV.

Open AndroBird 2026 →

In English, German and French. On a computer, open the Sightings tab and load the example data, a morning survey walk at Haff Réimech. It keeps its sightings separate from the 2012 version.
