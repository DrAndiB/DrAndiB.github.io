Roman News Isochrones

How many days did a message need to cross the Roman Empire? Pick a starting city, a messenger and a season, and the map colours the Empire by travel time. Tap a city to see the fastest route by road and sea. The model is checked against journeys recorded by Cicero, Plutarch, Tacitus and Pliny.
